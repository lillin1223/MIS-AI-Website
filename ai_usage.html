Correct AI Usage

The correct AI usage should be when your professor allows it or you are genuinely using it do better your education. AI has the option to create quizzes for you, which are very helpful for studying. AI can also help create calendars, study guides, and to-do lists. Some professors will let you use AI, as long as it is cited and follows the guidelines.

Your paragraph makes good points about using AI responsibly in college. I agree that practice quizzes, study guides, and calendars can help students learn and stay organized. One thing I would add is that students should not rely on AI for every assignment because it could prevent them from learning the material themselves. Students should also double-check information from AI because it can sometimes give incorrect answers.

Benefits of AI

  1. AI helps students by creating practice quizzes
  2. AI can explain difficult topics to help students understand them.

Who Benefits?

  1. College students who use AI to study and understand better.
  2. Professors who allow students to use AI for educational purposes.

How AI Tools Used

  • Students use AI to create study guides and practice quizzes.
  • AI can help students organize assignments and create to-do lists.

Incorrect AI Usage

Cheating on Assignments
Students use AI to complete assignments without doing the work.

Using AI on Exams
Students use AI to find answers during exams when it's not allowed.

Trusting Incorrect Information
Students rely on AI answers without checking if it's right.

Incorrect AI usage is unfair and will not further your education. If you are using AI to cheat on assignments/tests, you are not learning anything. AI should be used to help you study better or even give you a practice quiz, not bypass learning. Solely relying on AI is setting yourself up for failure because you retain no knowledge.
