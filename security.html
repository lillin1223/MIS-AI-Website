Malware and Cyber Threats

Yahoo Hacking Incident

The Yahoo data breach happened in 2013, over 3 billion accounts were breached. The attackers used spear phishing and forged web cookies to steal passwords, personal info, and sensitive financial data. The impact of this resulted in Yahoos loss of users, public trust and millions of dollars stolen. This was significant in cyber security because 3 billion people were affected.
